Who We Are

People

A collaborative team of researchers, engineers, and designers working at the intersection of cognitive science, machine learning, and educational technology.

Principal Investigator
PC
Paulo Carvalho, PhD
Assistant Professor
Human-Computer Interaction Institute · Carnegie Mellon University

Paulo leads the OAK Lab, where he investigates how computational models of learning can be grounded in cognitive and motivational research. His work spans controlled laboratory experiments, large-scale data analytics, and the design of AI-powered educational technologies deployed in real classroom settings.
