Our Mission

Learning science meets artificial intelligence

The OAK Lab sits at the intersection of cognitive science, machine learning, and educational technology. We study how people learn, build models that capture those processes, and design AI-powered interventions that make education more effective and equitable.

From controlled laboratory experiments to large-scale classroom deployments, we translate evidence-based findings into technology that actually works in the real world.
