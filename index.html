We study how people learn through practice, and build that science into adaptive learning technology tested at scale — from K-12 and college classrooms to rural Uganda.

Human-Computer Interaction Institute · Carnegie Mellon University

Our Research Publications

Research

Learning Mechanisms

We examine how learners infer abstract rules, regulate their learning, and retain knowledge over time — focusing on practice-based approaches rather than passive instruction.

Intelligent & Adaptive Systems

We develop computational models of learning and AI-driven systems that customize instruction, combining learning science, data analytics, and human-centered design to personalize experiences at scale.

Learning at Scale

We translate research findings into classroom interventions and learning technologies that work across different institutions, languages, and cultural environments.
