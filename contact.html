Collaboration

Partner with us

The OAK Lab actively collaborates with schools, industry partners, and other academic groups. We are especially interested in partnerships that allow us to deploy and evaluate learning technologies in real-world settings.

Schools & Districts

We partner with K-12 schools to deploy and study classroom interventions. Contact us to discuss research collaborations.

Industry & EdTech

Interested in grounding your product in learning science research? We welcome conversations about research partnerships and technology evaluation.

Academic Collaborators

We collaborate widely across cognitive science, HCI, and AI in education. Feel free to reach out to discuss shared interests.

Opportunities

Join the Lab

We welcome researchers at all career stages who are passionate about understanding how people learn and building technology that makes learning better.

Undergraduate & Master's Students

We regularly take on undergraduate and master's students as research assistants. Projects range from running behavioral experiments to developing machine learning models. If you're a CMU student interested in learning science research, we'd love to have you on the team.

Apply via Google Form →
PhD Students

Prospective PhD students should apply through the CMU Human-Computer Interaction Institute doctoral program. The lab encourages interested applicants to reach out to Paulo directly before applying to discuss research fit and mutual interests.

Email Paulo →
Postdoctoral Fellows

The lab welcomes inquiries from researchers interested in postdoctoral positions. We are particularly excited to hear from candidates who bring expertise in learning science, cognitive psychology, educational data mining, or AI in education.
