What We Do

Research

We develop computational models of learning informed by cognitive and motivational research, using experiments and data analytics to understand learning behavior, and leveraging AI to design personalized, practice-oriented learning environments.

Research Area 01
Learning Mechanisms: Practice, Feedback & Discovery

How do learners acquire knowledge through hands-on engagement rather than passive reception? This research thread investigates the cognitive mechanisms underlying effective learning — including how learners infer abstract rules, regulate their own learning processes, and consolidate knowledge into long-term memory.

We run behavioral experiments that carefully isolate when specific types of practice, feedback, and discovery experiences produce lasting comprehension versus superficial performance.

Team: Michael Asher Julia Conti Gillian Gold

Research Area 02
Intelligent & Adaptive Learning Systems

This research thread creates computational models that power personalized, AI-driven learning platforms. We combine learning science, data analysis, and user-centered design to model how knowledge is represented in the learner, generate customized practice sequences, and scale personalized instruction to many students simultaneously.

Our work spans knowledge component modeling, spacing and repetition algorithms, and the use of small language models to make intelligent tutoring more accessible across diverse contexts.

Research Area 03
Learning at Scale & Across Contexts

Research findings only matter if they help real students in real classrooms. This thread translates our evidence-based models into classroom interventions and educational technologies deployed in schools across diverse communities — including low-infrastructure and remote settings.

We emphasize accessibility, inclusion, and rigorous measurement of impact. By partnering directly with schools, we ensure our work is both scientifically grounded and practically meaningful.
